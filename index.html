Our Core Mission

Why SurplusSync Exists

Every day, tons of perfectly edible food from banquet events, corporate cafeterias, and restaurants are discarded into municipal landfills, while thousands in local communities suffer food insecurity.

Zero Hunger (SDG 2)

Ensuring wholesome, freshly prepared surplus meals reach local food banks, soup kitchens, and women's shelters within hours of preparation.

Methane & Carbon Reduction

Diverting organic waste from decomposing in landfills, directly preventing 2.5 kg of CO₂ equivalent greenhouse gas for every single kilogram rescued.

Certified Food Safety

Enforcing mandatory temperature controls (hot >60°C, chilled <4°C), packaging hygiene attestations, and strict real-time expiry countdowns.

Technical Excellence

What Makes Our Platform Different

Unlike static bulletin boards or generic donation directories, SurplusSync is an active logistical coordinator powered by modern web standards.

Real-Time Geospatial Matching

Interactive Leaflet maps calculate distance radii, pinpointing exact proximity between donating restaurants and shelters within 5 to 60 km.

6-Digit Cryptographic Handshake (OTP)

Zero lost deliveries. Volunteer couriers cannot mark food as collected without entering the donor's 6-digit PIN, nor delivered without the NGO's PIN.

Mandatory NGO Certificate Verification

To prevent fraudulent claims, NGOs must upload official government/charity registration certificates for administrator review before receiving surplus.

AI Smart Food Extraction Assistant

Busy chefs can paste natural kitchen notes or banquet menus; our NLP parser auto-fills quantity, unit, packaging, and shelf life instantly.

Live Environmental Impact Accounting

Dynamic dashboards calculate cumulative meals served, water conserved, and equivalent trees planted with visual SVG trend charts.

Append-Only Audit Governance

Every single listing, reservation, handover, and delivery is logged into an immutable audit trail with export to CSV for regulatory compliance.

Redistribution Lifecycle

How It Works in 4 Simple Steps

A seamless four-stage pipeline that takes surplus from commercial kitchens to shelter dining tables.

01

1. Donor Lists Food

Restaurants, caterers, or bakeries list surplus portions with safety certifications and consumption windows in under 2 minutes.

02

2. NGO Discovers & Claims

Verified shelters with approved registration certificates discover listings on proximity maps and reserve food with 1 click.

03

3. Volunteer Pickup

Eco-couriers accept the dispatch mission, navigate to the kitchen, and perform the 6-digit OTP verification handshake.

04

4. Community Fed

The shelter confirms receipt with the delivery PIN, meals are safely distributed, and sustainability analytics update in real time.
